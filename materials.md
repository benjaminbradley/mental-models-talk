# Materials

What to bring or provide. Derived from the exercises in `presentation-arc.md`.
Update as exercises are locked.

## Confirmed

- Paper (plain sheets) and pens/pencils, enough for every participant.
  - Describe-and-draw exercise (station 3): the "A" partner needs a printed source
    figure; the "B" partner needs blank paper to draw on.
  - Verification-asymmetry sort (station 5): each person writes ~5 real work tasks.

## Likely / TBD (confirm as exercises lock)

- Projector or large screen — for pre-captured transcripts (station 4 confabulation/
  jaggedness, station 7 guardrail probe) and the goldfish visuals.
- Printed describe-and-draw source figure(s) for the "A" partners (station 3).
- Anonymous entry & exit poll method — paper slips or a phone-based tool. Anonymity
  is the point (a show of hands measures conformity). See polls bookmark in
  `open-questions-and-ideas.md`.
- Printed one-page takeaway card — the self-diagnostic, the verification asymmetry,
  and the lethal trifecta. The only thing that persists after the room empties.
- Station 6c handout — the described "assistant with mailbox access" scenario for
  the small-group injection exercise.
- Station 8 handout — a plausible, polished, subtly-wrong artifact for the
  "would you sign it?" exercise.
- Timer / clock the facilitator can see.

## Open format dependencies (affect the above)

- Can participants pair up? Is there a screen? Network access? Do participants have
  their own devices? (See open questions in `open-questions-and-ideas.md`.)
