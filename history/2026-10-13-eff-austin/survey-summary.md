# Survey summary - 2026-10-13 EFF-Austin

Per the analysis plan in `planning/surveys.md`. Excludes the 2026-10-11 test rows
(1 entry, 1 exit). Raw data: `surveys/`.

## Response counts

- Entry: 21 responses (of ~28 attendees). Two identical "#alignedAI" answers 50 minutes
  apart are likely one person submitting twice; one response arrived at 9:11 PM, after the talk.
- Exit: **4 responses** (~14%). Too few to support conclusions; the read below is anecdotal.
  The check-out QR was only on the closing slide - see `planning/todo.md` (take-home card QR).

so the 4-point grid leaves no room to show movement for experienced users.

## Takeaways reported (exit Q4, recall probe)

- "Organized"/"conceptual framework" and "specific terms" for things already vaguely known
  (2) - matches the take-home card's framing ("each is a search term for going deeper").
- How LLMs work / making them work for you / doing it safely (1).
- Practical overview (1).
- Named elements that landed: the fish metaphor; the "tag" concept in the slides.
- No respondent named a specific method from the take-home card; with n=4, this says
  little about which methods stuck.

## Confusion and open questions (exit Q5, Q6)

- Wants to talk further about specifics of working safely (1).
- "Got a little disorganized in the middle and on but lucid throughout" (1) - the only
  critical note; worth checking the middle stations' transitions.

## Curiosities brought in (entry Q2) - input for the next revision

Grouped; counts are responses touching the theme.

- **Security**: hacking, LLMs as attack vectors, safe integration (3-4)
- **Better / effective use, new ideas**: better results, effective means, uses not yet
  imagined, technical problem-solving, limited vs. everything use (5-6)
- **Advanced practice**: niche practices, benchmarks vs. personal use, harnesses,
  customization, self-hosting; learning to vibe code (2)
- **Truth / trust**: truth determination; helping stakeholders see "they're not magic" (2)
- **Society**: jobs, sustainability, legislation, aligned AI (4)
- General knowledge; "insights from people who actually know" (2)

## Implications to consider

- The room skewed experienced; security and advanced practice (harnesses, agents,
  self-hosting) drew more curiosity than basics. Weigh against the talk's newcomer-inclusive
  goal per venue.
- Exit response rate is the main data gap: put the check-out QR on the take-home card and
  leave time for it on the closing slide.
- Consider a 5-point grid, or a "learned something new" item, so experienced users can
  register movement.
