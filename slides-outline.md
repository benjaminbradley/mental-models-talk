# Slides Outline

(speaker notes / narration), and any animation or interaction. Organized by station;
slide numbering is deferred until the deck is assembled.

Visual language and slide tech (HTML/CSS, presenter view, etc.) are tracked in
`open-questions-and-ideas.md` (Q8). This doc describes *content*; final visual

## Pre-talk

### Slide: Arrival / Welcome

- **Visual:** Talk title ("Mental Models for Using LLMs" or final title), speaker
  name and affiliation, large QR code linking to the anonymous entry survey.
- **Purpose:** On screen as people walk in and settle. No narration; the QR code
  gives early arrivals something to do.
- **Notes:** Survey design is tracked in open-questions (polls bookmark). QR code
  must be large enough to scan from the back of the room.

## Station 1 - Disarm + Acknowledge

### Slide: The real problems

- **Visual:** A series of images representing real harms - data centers, water
  usage, labor displacement, training-data provenance, concentration of power.
- **Animation:** Images drop into the frame one by one, then slide together into a
  box that closes and is set aside (visually: acknowledged, not dismissed, but
  boxed for now).
- **Narration:** "There are a lot of problems with the AI industry right now."
  Name each one briefly as the image appears. Then: "That is not what this talk
  is about." The box closes.
  Metaphor for station 1: Do cars kill people everyday yes do they pollute the s*** out of the environment everyday but I'm still going to teach you how to drive.
- **Tone:** Matter-of-fact, not dismissive. The box stays visible (corner? footer?)
  as a reminder that these were named, not hidden.

### Slide: What this talk IS about

- **Visual:** Clean text slide (or minimal imagery). Three bullet-like statements,
  revealed in sequence.
- **Narration:**
  - "This talk is about how LLMs work, and how to work with them safely and
    effectively."
  - "If you do not know what the big deal is - hopefully this will open your eyes
    to their potential."
  - "If you have worked with LLMs before - hopefully this will deepen your
    understanding."
  - "And if you are anti-AI - hopefully this will help you understand your enemy
    better."
- **Notes:** The third line is the skeptic recruitment from the arc. Deliver it
  with a smile, not an edge. This is the contract: literacy, not conversion.
