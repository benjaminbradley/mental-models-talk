# Take-Home Card

Rules for the card participants leave with. The card itself (content, print layout, build
command) is `take-home.typ`.

- **Scope:** covers what the talk teaches, and nothing it doesn't. Update the card when a method
  or principle is added, removed, or renamed in the talk.
- **Format:** term + a short memory-jogging phrase, one line per item. The term is what people
  search later; the phrase is a reminder, not an explanation.
- **Links:** QR codes for the slides and the contact page, plus the contact email. The email may
  appear on the printout but must not be a literal, scrapable string in the source.
